# 3.10 - ARCELI 1S–8S LiPo low-voltage alarm (ASIN B077Z1N9CS, 2-pack)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Cells | 1S–8S (2S–8S for the alarm function) |
| Dimensions | 40 × 25 × 11 mm, 10 g |
| Accuracy | ±0.01 V |
| Per-cell display | 0.5–4.5 V · pack total 0.5–36 V |
| Alarm threshold | adjustable 3.0–3.9 V in 0.1 V steps via rear button, or off |
| Connection | balance lead |

**Set it to 3.3–3.5 V/cell.** This is the layer that still works when the software is
broken, which during bring-up is most of the time. Keep it fitted permanently even after
the STM32 monitoring service is running.
