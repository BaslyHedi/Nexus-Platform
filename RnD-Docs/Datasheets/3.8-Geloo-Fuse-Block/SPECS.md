**Why this part:** the negative bus is what makes it a distribution *board* rather than a
fuse holder. Every return lands on one bar, which is the star ground for the whole vehicle.

**Considered and rejected:** FULARR 6-way (B07GXCR732), 115 × 53 × 42 mm. Narrower, but
only 8 % less deck area, no negative bus, no blown-fuse LEDs, 6.3 mm spade terminals
instead of screws, and 12 mm taller - worse on the deck gap, which is the tighter constraint.
